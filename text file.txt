Determining if a Finger Is Pressing

The z coordinate from MediaPipe alone isn't reliable enough to determine contact.

A better approach is to combine cues:

fingertip position relative to the fretboard plane
finger joint angles (a pressing finger is usually bent)
temporal stability across several frames
optional distance from the estimated fretboard plane

This is much more robust than using z alone.

Why I think this architecture is the best fit

Your project doesn't need to recognize arbitrary guitars in uncontrolled environments—it needs to provide accurate feedback for a user willing to place a few colored markers on the instrument. By using those markers to establish the fretboard's pose, you avoid training a custom guitar detector entirely. MediaPipe handles the hand, OpenCV handles the guitar geometry, and your application logic only needs to reason about finger positions in the fretboard's coordinate system. This approach is simpler to implement, easier to debug, and should be capable of running in real time on a phone.
